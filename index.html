Reference Equations

▾

Reference material — kept in sync with js/physics.js: the period (Kepler’s 3rd law), vis-viva speed and the drawn orbit shape (Kepler’s 1st law, used for the dashed reference ellipse) are evaluated in code. Kepler’s equation is solved in js/kepler.js (Stage 3.1) and Kepler’s 2nd law is checked numerically in js/areas.js (Stage 3.2, equal-area panel). The planet position itself always comes from numerical Verlet integration, not a Kepler solver.

Kepler’s 1st law — orbit shape
r = a(1 − e²) / (1 + e·cos ν)
Kepler’s 2nd law — equal areas
dA/dt = ½ r² (dν/dt) = const
Kepler’s 3rd law — period
T² = (4π² / GM) · a³
Kepler’s equation
M = E − e·sin E
Vis-viva equation
v² = GM (2/r − 1/a)
